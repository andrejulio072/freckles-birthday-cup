A letter from Andre

The gym misses your laugh.

♥ To Freckles ↓

Freckles, I still laugh when I look at the photos from that shift swap. I told you I’d swap if you wore my PT shirt, and once you put it on it looked like a dress. How we went from that to taking photos with my car and a stair railing, I still don’t know.

Every time I struggled to park when you were on shift, I’d picture you in the office pissing yourself laughing at me. I still think of you when I put the car back in reverse for another go, and by then I’m usually laughing too.

What I really miss is coming over to tell you about a date or some stupid thing that happened to me and seeing you stop what you were doing because you wanted to hear the rest. I’d come in with a new episode every week, and you’d still listen even when my supposed two-minute story took three detours before I got to what actually happened.

I loved working with you, Lauren, and I still think, “Wait till I tell her this,” whenever something ridiculous happens. I know the people at the gym miss hearing you laugh, and I miss being there laughing with you. I’m sorry I got this to you a day late, but I hope it gives you a laugh anyway. Happy belated birthday, Freckles. I’m really glad we met.

Andre ♡

Still trying to park ✦

When we worked together, I used to imagine you watching from the office while I tried to park. Now whenever I have to pull out and try again, I still picture you laughing at me, which is usually enough to get me laughing too.

Previously on Andre’s Life ♥

I used to come over with an update on my love life or whatever ridiculous thing I’d done that week, and you’d stop what you were doing to hear the whole story. I swear, sometimes I was more excited to tell you how the date went than I was to go on it in the first place.

“Quick story” ✿

I’d tell you it was a quick story, but then I’d remember you needed the backstory, which obviously had its own backstory. By the time I got to the point, we’d usually be laughing at some completely irrelevant detail and I’d have to ask you where I was.

The PT shirt dress ✦

When I said I’d swap shifts if you wore my PT shirt, I thought I’d get a laugh out of seeing you in it. It looked like a dress on you, and somehow my car and the stair railing ended up in the photos too. I still laugh thinking about how much came out of me asking you to put on one shirt.

Season two ♥

I still catch myself thinking I need to tell you what just happened, so by the time we catch up you’re going to need a recap. I’d promise to keep it short, but after all those stories at the gym I don’t think either of us would believe me.
