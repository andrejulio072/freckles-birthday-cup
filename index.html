A letter from Andre

The gym misses your laugh.

♥ To Freckles ↓

Lauren, remember when I said I’d swap the shift if you wore my PT shirt? It looked like a dress on you. I was expecting you to put it on for a laugh. Somehow there’s a whole photo shoot, my car gets a cameo, and the stair railing makes it into the campaign. I should’ve asked for a shift swap more often.

Every time you were on shift and I was parking, I’d imagine you in the office watching me make a three-point turn into a seven-point turn. I still do it now. As soon as I have to pull out and try again, I think, “Freckles would be pissing herself right now.”

I miss the office debriefs too. Sometimes it was a date that went sideways, sometimes it was just something stupid I’d done. Halfway through telling you, I’d already be laughing at myself. People at the gym miss that energy. I miss hearing you laugh from across the floor and thinking, “Right, wait till she hears this one.” Working with you was a real pleasure, Lauren.

I’m sorry this didn’t reach you on your actual birthday. I hope you had a lovely day. I’m really glad we met, Freckles. Next time I see you, I owe you a proper catch-up. I’ll park before you get there.

Andre ♡

Parking, judged by Freckles ✦

You’ve left the gym and somehow you’re still judging my parking from the office. I can hear the laugh before I’ve even put the handbrake on.

The dating debriefs ♥

Some of those dates were a write-off. The retelling on shift with you was easily the best part of the whole thing.

Best laugh in the gym ✿

Some days I could hear you laughing from across the gym. I’d know two things straight away: you were in, and whatever I’d come over to tell you was about to take twice as long.

The shift-swap dress ✦

“I’ll swap the shift if you wear my PT shirt.” You wore it. It looked like a dress. The photos are still my favourite thing to come out of a rota change.

The next shift swap ♥

Next time you need me to swap, you can pick what I wear. I realise I’ve just given you far too much power.
