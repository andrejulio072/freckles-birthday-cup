A letter from Andre

The gym misses your laugh.

♥ To Freckles ↓

Lauren, I loved working shifts with you. I don’t think I ever told you this, but whenever you were on shift and I was struggling to park, I’d picture you in the office watching me and laughing. Even now, if I mess up getting into a space, I think, “Freckles would be laughing at me right now.” And then I start laughing at myself too.

I miss our debriefs as well. I’d come in with another chapter of my ridiculous love life, a date that went sideways, or just some stupid thing that happened to me, and somehow we’d both end up laughing about it. I could tell you the whole messy story, and by the time I’d finished, it felt lighter.

People at the gym miss the energy you brought to a shift. I miss hearing your laugh from across the gym and knowing I’d have someone to tell my latest disaster to. Getting to work with you was a real pleasure.

I’m sorry this didn’t reach you on your actual birthday. I hope you had a lovely day, and I hope this gives you a bit of the joy you gave us. Happy belated birthday, Freckles. I’m really glad we met.

Andre ♡
